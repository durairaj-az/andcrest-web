Services

ServiceNow services, end to end

From the first roadmap workshop to a platform your teams run themselves, Andcrest covers the full ServiceNow journey in partnership with you.

01 / Roadmap before build

Consulting & Advisory

We define the ServiceNow roadmap and business case before any build begins, so the platform investment tracks your business goals and not just a feature list.

  • Landscape analysis of your current IT and business tooling
  • Custom adoption and migration roadmap
  • Business case, prioritisation and ROI optimisation
  • Platform governance and operating model design
Key outcome. A clear, costed plan that your leadership and delivery teams can both follow.
02 / Aligned delivery, clean platform

Implementation & Integration

We deploy ServiceNow modules with your teams, using platform best practices and minimal customisation, so upgrades stay simple and the design is ready to integrate.

  • Co-delivery aligned to your business functions
  • Out-of-the-box first, with portal and UX design for employees and customers
  • Integrations with ERP, CRM, identity and cloud platforms
  • Testing, go-live and post-implementation support
Key outcome. A scalable ServiceNow foundation that goes live on plan and stays upgrade-safe.
03 / Your ServiceNow Centre of Excellence

Managed Services

After go-live we keep the platform healthy and moving. A dedicated team acts as your Centre of Excellence for support, upgrades and a steady stream of enhancements.

  • Incident, request and release support
  • Release upgrades and platform health checks
  • Enhancement backlog delivered in regular sprints
  • Dashboards and reports that track performance and value
Key outcome. Stable operations and continuous improvement without building a large in-house team.
04 / Intelligent workflows

AI & Automation

We build AI-driven workflows on the platform: virtual agents, generative AI integrations and AIOps that help your teams resolve issues before they escalate.

  • Virtual Agent and conversational self-service
  • Generative AI integrations in service workflows
  • AIOps for predictive issue handling
  • Automation of repetitive approvals and fulfilment
Key outcome. Faster resolution and less manual work across IT, HR and customer service.
05 / Custom workflows, well designed

Workflow Apps & Experience

Where no system exists yet, we build low-code applications on App Engine and shape the portals around them, so each business function gets a workflow that is simple to use.

  • Custom apps for HR, finance, facilities, legal and procurement
  • Employee Center and customer portal design, with UI/UX built in
  • Approvals, forms and fulfilment automated end to end
  • Governed low-code development your own team can extend
Key outcome. New business processes live on the platform quickly, with an experience people choose to use.
06 / Adoption that lasts

Organisational Change Management

A platform only delivers value when people use it. We plan communication, training and handover alongside the technical work.

  • Stakeholder analysis and change planning
  • Role-based training and documentation
  • Champions network and feedback loops
  • Handover so your team can run the platform
Key outcome. Teams that are ready on day one and confident to own the platform.
