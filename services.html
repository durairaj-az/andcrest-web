Services

ServiceNow consulting services

From the first roadmap workshop to a platform your teams run themselves, Andcrest advises on the full ServiceNow journey.

Roadmap before build

Consulting & Advisory

We define the ServiceNow roadmap and business case before any build begins, so the platform investment tracks your business goals and not just a feature list.

  • Landscape analysis of your current IT and business tooling
  • Custom adoption and migration roadmap
  • Business case, prioritisation and ROI optimisation
  • Platform governance and operating model design
Key outcome. A clear, costed plan that your leadership and delivery teams can both follow.
Independent guidance through delivery

Implementation Advisory

We guide your teams and your chosen implementers through delivery: solution architecture, design reviews and integration planning, so the platform stays clean and upgrade-safe.

  • Solution architecture and design authority support
  • Out-of-the-box first, with portal and UX guidance for employees and customers
  • Integration design for ERP, CRM, identity and cloud platforms
  • Delivery assurance, testing strategy and go-live readiness
Key outcome. A scalable ServiceNow foundation that goes live on plan and stays upgrade-safe.
Keep the platform healthy and valuable

Governance & Optimisation

After go-live we help you run ServiceNow as a product: health checks, release planning, and a governed backlog that keeps the platform delivering value.

  • Platform health checks and technical debt review
  • Release upgrade planning and readiness
  • Enhancement backlog prioritisation and roadmap
  • Dashboards and reports that track performance and value
Key outcome. Stable operations and continuous improvement without building a large in-house team.
Intelligent workflows

AI & Automation Advisory

We help you decide where AI belongs on the platform: virtual agents, generative AI and AIOps, with clear use cases, guardrails and a plan to prove value.

  • Virtual Agent and conversational self-service strategy
  • Generative AI use cases for service workflows
  • AIOps opportunities for predictive issue handling
  • Automation of repetitive approvals and fulfilment
Key outcome. Faster resolution and less manual work across IT, finance and customer service.
App Engine: custom workflows

Workflow & Experience Design

Build your own application on ServiceNow. We help you choose the right App Engine tools for each need, then design the workflow and the user experience around them.

  • App Engine: the core low-code and pro-code platform to build, test and deploy enterprise applications
  • Creator Studio: a guided no-code space for business teams to digitise form-based requests, approvals and workflows
  • Workflow Studio: one hub to design, build and manage flows, subflows and action logic
  • IntegrationHub: connect your workflows to external systems, APIs and data sources
  • Now Assist for Creator: generative AI that turns text into code, generates flows and recommends next steps to speed up development
Key outcome. Business teams get purpose-built applications on one governed App Engine platform, built faster with the right mix of no-code, low-code and pro-code, and connected to the systems around them.
Adoption that lasts

Organisational Change Management

A platform only delivers value when people use it. We plan communication, training and handover alongside the technical work.

  • Stakeholder analysis and change planning
  • Role-based training and documentation
  • Champions network and feedback loops
  • Handover so your team can run the platform
Key outcome. Teams that are ready on day one and confident to own the platform.
